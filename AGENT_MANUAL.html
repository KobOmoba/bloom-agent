Chapter 01

What Is This App?

The short answer: it's your toolkit for visiting schools, counting students, and sending deals to Bayo for approval — all from your phone.

The Edu-BLOOM Agent App is a mobile web application for field agents. You use it when you visit a school, speak to the Principal, and want to sign them up to the Edu-BLOOM Premium platform. Everything from scanning the school signboard to submitting the deal is done inside this one app.

The app lives at agent.edubloom.com.ng and works like a normal website, but it can be added to your home screen so it opens like an installed app — no Play Store or App Store needed.

📋

💳

✨
Premium Plans Select the right plan based on student count. All plans are now full Premium.
📊

💰
20% Commission Every approved deal earns you 20% of the term fee, paid to your registered bank account.
Important: This is the live production app. Deals you submit here go directly to Bayo's portal and affect real schools. Always verify everything on a real device before closing an interaction.

Section 1 — Scan School Signboard

Photograph the school signboard and the app fills in the school name, address, LGA, and state automatically.

This is the first thing to do when you arrive at a school. Tap the 📸 Scan School Signboard button at the top of the New Deal screen.

1
Point your camera at the signboard The full board should be visible and the text should be legible. Good lighting helps a lot — avoid harsh shadows across the text.
2
Take the photo Tap the shutter button. You can also upload a photo from your gallery if you already took one.
3
Wait a few seconds The app sends the photo to the AI engine. You will see a "Reading signboard…" status message. This usually takes 3–8 seconds.
4
Review and correct the filled fields School Name, Address, LGA, and State are auto-filled from the signboard text. Tap any field to correct it if the AI made a mistake.

Chapter 06

Section 2 — Smart Register Counter

Count the school's students accurately — by photographing the register, or by pasting a list of names.

The Smart Register Counter lets you find out exactly how many students are enrolled, which determines the Premium Plan tier and your commission. There are two ways to use it.

Option A — Photograph the Register

1
Tap "📷 Scan Register Photo" Your camera opens. Photograph the class register pages — multiple photos are supported, one per page.
2
Hold the phone steady over the page Good light and a straight angle give the best results. The app will warn you if the image is too blurry to read.
3
Wait for the count to appear The AI reads only student NAMES from the register (not fees or marks). The total count is shown below, and the correct plan tier is highlighted automatically.

Option B — Paste Names (CSV or plain text)

1
Tap the text / paste area You can paste a list of student names separated by commas, new lines, or as a CSV file from another source.
2
Tap Count The app parses the names and gives you a total count instantly — no AI needed for this method.
Important: This section counts students for tier selection only. It does NOT extract fees or payment status — that is what the Financial Ledger Scan (Section 3) is for.
Chapter 07

Section 3 — Financial Ledger Scan

Photograph the school fees ledger to see who has paid, who hasn't, and the outstanding fee total — class by class.

This is the most powerful feature of the app. A school's fees ledger lists every student alongside their payment status and amounts. Scanning it lets you show the Principal exactly how much money is outstanding — making your pitch far stronger.

What the app reads from the ledger: Student name · Payment status (PAID, PARTIAL, or UNCLEAR) · Term fees · Balance outstanding. It processes multiple pages at once and groups results by class.

Scanning the Ledger

1
Tap "📷 Add Ledger Page" Photograph one page of the fees ledger. You can add as many pages as needed — the app processes them all together.
2
Keep each page straight and well-lit The app automatically corrects for slight angles (perspective correction) and removes blur, but extreme angles will reduce accuracy. Aim for a flat, overhead shot.
3
Tap "📖 Read All Pages" The app processes every page one by one through the AI cascade. A live feed shows progress page by page. Pages with many students may take 10–15 seconds each.
4
Review the grouped results Results are organised by class group. Each group shows total students, paid count, and outstanding amount. Any pages that failed to read are flagged — tap "Retry Failed Pages" to try again.
UNCLEAR status: When the AI cannot confidently read a student's payment status (e.g. handwriting is smudged), it marks them as UNCLEAR. These students are not counted as owing — only confirmed unpaid students appear in the outstanding total. This protects you from showing a falsely high figure to the Principal.

Tips & Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to fix them quickly in the field.

📵 The app says "Firebase permission error" when I log in
This is a server-side issue, not your fault. Contact Bayo immediately — the Firestore security rules may need to be updated. Nothing you do on your phone will fix this.
📷 The signboard scan came back with the wrong name or address
The AI is accurate but not perfect on poorly lit or distant signs. Simply tap the field and correct it manually — the form is always editable regardless of what the scan found.
⏳ The OCR is taking very long or showing "Rate limit"
The AI engines have usage limits. When one is full, the app automatically waits and tries the next one in the cascade. Do not close the app — just wait. It will complete on its own.
📖 Some ledger pages say "Failed" in the results
This can happen with very blurry, dark, or heavily tilted photos. Tap "Retry Failed Pages" — the app retries only the failed pages, not the ones that already worked. If it fails again, retake the photo of that specific page in better light.
🔢 The page counter on the ledger shows "6/5" or wrong numbers
This was a known display bug — it has been fixed as of July 2026. If you see it, clear your browser cache (Settings → Clear browsing data) and reload the app to get the latest version.
🎨 The app looks light/pastel instead of dark navy
A stale cached version is loading. Clear site data for agent.edubloom.com.ng in your browser settings, then reload. The dark navy theme is correct.
📜 The New Agent registration page won't scroll back up
This has been fixed (August 17, 2026). Clear your browser cache or use incognito mode to force the updated stylesheet to load — the page will scroll freely in both directions.
📤 I submitted a deal but it's not showing up in Bayo's portal
If you were offline when you submitted, the deal may be queued for sync. Wait until you are back on a good connection — the app will sync automatically. If you had a signal when submitting and still cannot see it, contact Bayo directly.
💰 My commission figure looks wrong or is missing
Commission is recorded by Bayo's portal when he approves a deal. If the deal is still "Pending" it will not yet show in Earnings. If it is "Approved" and the figure is missing, contact Bayo on +234 814 507 3941.

Best Practices in the Field

💡
Scan the signboard first, always It fills in all the school details in one shot. Starting with the scan saves you from typing a long school name manually.
💡
Ask to see the fees ledger Principals who see the outstanding fees figure are much more likely to sign up. Ask politely — it's a simple record they keep at the school.
💡
Use Show Principal before submitting Let the Principal confirm all the details are correct before you submit. This avoids rejection due to wrong school information.
💡
Stay on a good network during submission The deal submission writes directly to the database. A dropped connection mid-submit can create a stuck deal. Wait for the green confirmation before leaving the school.
